Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TimeKu ("the App") handles information when you use the application. By using TimeKu, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Process

TimeKu may process information that you enter or select while using the App, including:

  • Timer, stopwatch, and alarm settings.
  • Saved timer history and preferences.
  • Text used for text-to-speech (TTS) announcements.
  • Alarm sound files (such as MP3 files) selected by you.
  • Photos or videos you choose to capture or save, if those features are used.

The App may also receive technical information from third-party services, such as advertising or diagnostic information, as described below.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and maintain App features, including:

  • Saving your timer, stopwatch, and alarm preferences.
  • Running alarms and playing sounds selected by you.
  • Providing TTS announcements based on your settings.
  • Saving or backing up content when you choose to use supported backup features.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements, where applicable.
  • Improving the stability and performance of the App.

3. Local Storage and Google Drive

Some settings and history may be stored locally on your device. If you choose to use a Google Drive backup or upload feature, the files you select may be transferred to your Google Drive account. Such transfers are initiated by you and are subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services

TimeKu may use third-party services to support certain features. These may include:

  • Google AdMob: to display advertisements. AdMob may collect or process information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive: if you choose to use backup or upload features, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

The services actually used may depend on the features enabled in your version of the App.

5. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, TimeKu may request access to device functions such as notifications, alarms, audio, camera, photos, videos, or files. Permissions are used only to support the related features. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to deliver features you use, such as advertising or cloud backup. Information you choose to upload to Google Drive is handled by Google under its own policies.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps within the App to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Data stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Backed-up files may remain in your Google Drive until you delete them from that account.

8. Children's Privacy

TimeKu is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Your Choices

You can change App settings and manage device permissions at any time. You can delete locally stored App data through the App, where available, or through your device settings. You are responsible for managing files that you have backed up to your Google Drive account.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
