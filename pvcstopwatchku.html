Privacy Policy

StopwatchKU — Multi-Activity Stopwatch

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StopwatchKU handles information when you use the app. Please read it carefully.

1. About StopwatchKU

StopwatchKU is a stopwatch and activity-recording application. Depending on the features you use, it can store activity names, timing records, group and participant information, notes, and photos or videos attached to activities.

2. Information the App Handles

  • Activity and timing information: activity names, categories, notes, elapsed times, recorded results, and group or participant details that you enter or create.
  • Photos and videos: media you choose to capture or select and attach to an activity.
  • Location information: if you choose to use a location feature and grant permission, the app may access the device's location to associate a location with an activity. Location access is optional.
  • App preferences: settings such as speech announcements and other preferences needed for the app to operate.

3. How Information Is Stored

Activity records, preferences, and media are designed to be stored on your device. StopwatchKU provides backup and restore features that create or restore a backup archive on your device. If you choose to share, send, or save a backup or exported report using another app or service, that destination's privacy practices will apply to the information you share.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored app data. Keep a backup of information you want to preserve.

4. Permissions

StopwatchKU may request these permissions when you use related features:

  • Camera: to take photos or record videos for an activity.
  • Photos, videos, or media access: to select and display media attached to activities.
  • Location: to obtain and save an optional location for an activity.
  • Notifications or alarms, where applicable: to provide reminders or related app functions.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. If you deny a permission, the feature that needs it may not be available, while other features may remain usable.

5. Text-to-Speech

StopwatchKU can use your device's text-to-speech service to announce timing information and activity status. Speech processing is handled by the text-to-speech engine configured on your device. That engine may be provided by your device manufacturer or another service provider and may be subject to its own privacy policy and settings.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

StopwatchKU may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its associated service providers may collect or receive information, such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related information, to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to your device settings, applicable consent requirements, and Google's policies.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how AdMob uses data.

7. Sharing of Information

StopwatchKU does not provide a feature for selling your activity records or personal media. Information may be shared when you explicitly choose to export or share a file, use another app to send a backup, or interact with third-party advertising services. Third-party apps and services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android's backup and device settings. You can delete activities and associated media using the app's available controls. You are responsible for deleting exported or shared copies from other apps, devices, or storage locations.

9. Children's Privacy

StopwatchKU is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact that service provider and contact us using the details below.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing the app to keep locally stored information within the app's storage area. However, no device, application, or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Protect your device and any backup files you create.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be made available at the policy URL, with a revised effective date.
